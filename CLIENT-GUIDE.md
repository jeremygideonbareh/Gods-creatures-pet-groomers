# 🐾 Gods Creatures Pet Groomers — Your Website Guide

## Your Website
**URL:** https://godscreaturespet.in

---

## How to Log In
1. Go to the website
2. Click **"Sign In"** (top-right corner)
3. Enter your email and password
4. Click **Sign In**

---

## How to Edit Website Content
1. Log in (see above)
2. Click the **🛡️ Admin Panel** button (top-left corner)
3. Click the **Content** tab
4. Choose what to edit:
   - **Hero** — main banner title, subtitle, video
   - **Why Choose Us** — the 4 reason cards
   - **Services** — grooming service descriptions & images
   - **Reviews** — customer testimonials & photos
   - **Booking** — contact info, hours, pricing text
   - **Backgrounds** — section background images
5. Make your changes
6. Click **"Save Changes"** (green checkmark appears when saved)

---

## How to View & Manage Bookings
1. Log in as admin
2. Click **Admin Panel** → **Bookings** tab
3. You'll see all customer booking requests
4. **Confirm** a booking by clicking the ✓ button
5. **Cancel** a booking if needed
